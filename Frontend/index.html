The Problem & Our Solution

The Problem

The agricultural supply chain faces significant challenges including:

  • Limited transparency in pricing and transactions
  • Unfair pricing practices affecting farmers' income
  • Fraud and counterfeit products in the market
  • Lack of traceability for food safety issues
  • Inefficient paperwork and manual record-keeping

Our Solution

FarmTrack addresses these challenges through:

  • Blockchain-based immutable record keeping
  • QR code integration for easy access to information
  • Transparent pricing and transaction history
  • Complete traceability from farm to consumer
  • Streamlined processes reducing paperwork

Project Context

FarmTrack was developed by team AGRI_TECH_IT for Smart India Hackathon 2025 to address critical issues in the agricultural supply chain using cutting-edge blockchain technology.

Challenges & Mitigation

Low Digital Literacy

Many farmers have limited experience with digital technologies.

Solution: Simple tools, intuitive interfaces, and comprehensive training programs.

Data Privacy Concerns

Stakeholders may be concerned about data security and privacy.

Solution: Permission-based data controls and transparent privacy policies.

Infrastructure Limitations

Rural areas may have limited internet connectivity.

Solution: Offline capabilities with sync when connectivity is available.

Adoption Resistance

Traditional stakeholders may resist changing established practices.

Solution: Demonstrate clear benefits and provide gradual transition pathways.

How FarmTrack Works

Discover the technical implementation and workflow of our blockchain-based supply chain solution

The FarmTrack Process

1

Farm Registration & Product Entry

Farmers register their agricultural produce in the system, providing details about cultivation practices, harvest date, and initial quality assessments. This information is recorded on the blockchain as the first immutable record.

2

QR Code Generation & Tagging

Each product batch receives a unique QR code that links to its blockchain record. This QR code is physically attached to the products, creating a digital-physical connection that follows the produce through the entire supply chain.

3

Supply Chain Movement & Verification

As products move from farmers to distributors, processors, retailers, and finally to consumers, each transaction and transfer is recorded on the blockchain. At each step, stakeholders verify the product's authenticity and update its status.

4

Consumer Access & Verification

Consumers can scan the QR code on the final product to access the complete history - from farm details to every step in the supply chain. This provides transparency and builds trust in the product's origin and quality.

5

Data Analytics & Insights

The collected data provides valuable insights for all stakeholders - farmers can understand market trends, distributors can optimize logistics, and governments can monitor food safety and supply chain efficiency.

Frequently Asked Questions

How can farmers join the FarmTrack system?

Farmers can register through our partner agricultural organizations or directly through our website. We provide training and support to help farmers get started with the system.

Is there a cost for farmers to use FarmTrack?

Basic FarmTrack services are free for small-scale farmers. Premium features with advanced analytics are available through subscription plans tailored to different farm sizes.

How does FarmTrack ensure data privacy?

We use permission-based data controls, encryption, and follow strict data privacy regulations. Farmers control what information is shared and with whom.

Can FarmTrack work in areas with limited internet connectivity?

Yes, FarmTrack has offline capabilities that allow data to be recorded locally and synchronized when internet connectivity is available.

How can retailers and distributors integrate with FarmTrack?

We provide API integration options and dedicated support for businesses looking to incorporate FarmTrack into their existing systems.
