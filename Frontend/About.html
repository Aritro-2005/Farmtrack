About FarmTrack

Learn about our mission to transform the agricultural supply chain with blockchain technology

The Problem & Our Solution

The Problem

The agricultural supply chain faces significant challenges including:

  • Limited transparency in pricing and transactions
  • Unfair pricing practices affecting farmers' income
  • Fraud and counterfeit products in the market
  • Lack of traceability for food safety issues
  • Inefficient paperwork and manual record-keeping

Our Solution

FarmTrack addresses these challenges through:

  • Blockchain-based immutable record keeping
  • QR code integration for easy access to information
  • Transparent pricing and transaction history
  • Complete traceability from farm to consumer
  • Streamlined processes reducing paperwork

Project Context

FarmTrack was developed by team AGRI_TECH_IT for Smart India Hackathon 2025 to address critical issues in the agricultural supply chain using cutting-edge blockchain technology.

Challenges & Mitigation

Low Digital Literacy

Many farmers have limited experience with digital technologies.

Solution: Simple tools, intuitive interfaces, and comprehensive training programs.

Data Privacy Concerns

Stakeholders may be concerned about data security and privacy.

Solution: Permission-based data controls and transparent privacy policies.

Infrastructure Limitations

Rural areas may have limited internet connectivity.

Solution: Offline capabilities with sync when connectivity is available.

Adoption Resistance

Traditional stakeholders may resist changing established practices.

Solution: Demonstrate clear benefits and provide gradual transition pathways.
