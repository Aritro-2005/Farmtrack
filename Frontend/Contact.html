Frequently Asked Questions

How can farmers join the FarmTrack system?

Farmers can register through our partner agricultural organizations or directly through our website. We provide training and support to help farmers get started with the system.

Is there a cost for farmers to use FarmTrack?

Basic FarmTrack services are free for small-scale farmers. Premium features with advanced analytics are available through subscription plans tailored to different farm sizes.

How does FarmTrack ensure data privacy?

We use permission-based data controls, encryption, and follow strict data privacy regulations. Farmers control what information is shared and with whom.

Can FarmTrack work in areas with limited internet connectivity?

Yes, FarmTrack has offline capabilities that allow data to be recorded locally and synchronized when internet connectivity is available.

How can retailers and distributors integrate with FarmTrack?

We provide API integration options and dedicated support for businesses looking to incorporate FarmTrack into their existing systems.
