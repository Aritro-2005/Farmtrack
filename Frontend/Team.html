Smart India Hackathon 2025

FarmTrack was developed as part of Smart India Hackathon 2025, a nationwide initiative to promote innovation and problem-solving among students and young professionals. Our project addresses the problem statement focused on improving transparency and efficiency in agricultural supply chains using emerging technologies.

We are proud to contribute to the digital transformation of India's agricultural sector and believe that FarmTrack has the potential to make a significant impact on farmer livelihoods and food security.
